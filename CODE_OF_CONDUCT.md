# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in the KSA Repository Health project, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with respect and patience.
- Give feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes responsibly.
- Keep technical discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Spam, impersonation, or deceptive activity
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to **alexmtrfnn@gmail.com**. Include relevant links or screenshots when available, but do not share unrelated personal or confidential information.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as reasonably possible. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is publicly representing the project or its community.
